# Setara documentation

Setara ("star" in Dari) is an offline learning app for cheap Linux game
handhelds. It is meant for village classrooms in Afghanistan: children of
about 6 to 14, many of whom cannot read yet, and teachers with little or no
English. Everything is spoken. It needs no internet and stores no personal
data. One device with a loudspeaker serves a teacher and a class of up to 50;
children can also play alone or in pairs. There are four subjects: English,
Dari reading, math, and health and environment.

**Where the project stands.** The app runs on a PC and in a browser. It has
not been run on a handheld yet. Nobody has listened to the generated speech,
and the voices are stand-ins (the Persian voice is Iranian, not Afghan). All
content was written by an AI (Claude) and has not been reviewed by a
Dari-speaking educator, a health professional or the local team. It must not
reach children before that review. See
[content-overview.md](content-overview.md) and
[privacy-and-safety.md](privacy-and-safety.md).
