# Review notes: what a human still has to decide

**Nothing in Setara's content has been reviewed by a native Dari speaker, a
teacher, a health professional or the local team.** It was written by an AI
(Claude) and then checked by further AI passes, which fixed errors and wrote
down what they could not judge. This file collects those open points, so
that the people who do the real review can start from them.

How to use it: take one section, sit with the sheets (`content/…`), the
English of every text (`content/i18n/*.csv`) and the app in English mode,
and decide each point. Change the sheets; the builder does the rest.

## For everyone

- **Listen to the speech.** No clip has been heard by a person. The voices
  are stand-ins; the Persian one is Iranian and male. Words that are spelled
  alike but said differently are a known risk: «نه» (no / nine), «خرد» (small
  / wisdom), «ده» (ten / village), «سر», «در». Nine and small are written with
  a vowel mark (نُه، خُرد) so that a voice reads them right; check each.
- **Look at every picture of a person** with someone who knows the place.
  Dress, headscarves, gestures (hand on chest for thank you, beckoning,
  pointing), girls and boys drawn together.
- **Words that differ by region.** The sheets use Afghan Dari as the authors
  knew it: مکتب، صنف، معلم، شفاخانه، کلکین، دروازه، بکس، چوکی، پشک، تشناب،
  بوره، مالته، کچالو، زردک، جواری، نصواری، چتل، خُرد/کلان، قطار (a row of
  pupils), جوره (a pair). Confirm or replace.
- **Ezafe** is written in some packs (جملهٔ) and not in others (کلمه آب).
  Pick one.
- **18** is written «هژده» and **19** «نزده». Some prefer «هجده».

## English 1 and 2, and the teacher lessons

- "boy" is «پسر» (not «بچه»); "no" is «نه»; "sad" is «غمگین»; "bag" is
  «بکس»; "classroom" is «اطاق صنف». Confirm.
- "Goodbye" is «خداحافظ». Confirm it is the wanted neutral form.
- Every lesson opens with "Good morning, class!". Classes in the afternoon?
- Lesson 7 (How old are you?): children older than ten can only say "ten",
  because level 1 counts to ten. Decide whether to teach 11 to 14 there.
- Lessons 4 and 14 ask the teacher to draw a boy and a girl on the board.
  Acceptable everywhere?
- Ball-passing tasks (lessons 3, 7, 16) have one child answer alone, with
  the class joining in if she is shy. Gentle enough?
- Lesson 13 asks the teacher to bring bread, water, an egg, an apple and
  tea. Board drawings are the fallback.
- Home phrases ask a child to put an English question to family who know no
  English.
- Plurals appear in shop and number rounds before unit 17 teaches them.
- Sound Catcher depends on how the voice says single words; listen.

## Dari reading 1 and 2

- **Letter names.** «طا» and «ظا» are used; many teachers say «طوی», «ظوی».
  ح and ه are both spoken "he": should they be «حِ جیمی» and «هِ دوچشمه»?
  Letter names are written with a vowel mark for the voice (بِ، تِ، رِ).
- آ is taught as its own symbol next to ا in level 1. Right?
- Key words for rare letters: واسکت (و), یخ (ی), ژاله, هیزم, حوض, حویلی,
  ثمر, ذره, ضعیف, ظرف.
- Rhymes hold only in Dari vowels: توت/بوت، سیب/جیب، زنبور/انگور،
  روباه/کلاه، چشم/پشم، گل/پل.
- The short-vowel round (level 2, round 13) depends on the voice honouring
  زبر، زیر، پیش, and shows non-words as wrong choices.
- "Syllable" is «بخش»; schools may say «هجا».
- Non-joining letters (ا د ذ ر ز ژ و) are sorted into "alone" and "end"
  only. Strictly the "end" shape also occurs in the middle of a word.
- Reading speeds in the curriculum are placeholders.

## Math 1 to 3

- Operation words: «جمع»، «منفی»، «ضرب»، «مساوی». Is «منفی» right for
  taking away, or «کم کن» / «تفریق»?
- Place value is said as «سه ده و چهار یک». Textbooks may say دهی‌ها،
  یکی‌ها.
- Fractions: «پوره / نیم / چارک». Even and odd: «جفت / طاق».
- Time: «ساعت سه است» or «سه بجه»?
- Prices in the market rounds are random numbers, not real prices.

## Health 1 and 2

A health professional should decide:

- Tap and hand-pump water is sorted as "clean" in one round. The round that
  says "drink now" no longer includes them, but the "clean" basket does.
- Boiling: "a strong rolling boil" with no minutes. Add a time for altitude?
- The diarrhoea story gives oral rehydration at home first and the clinic on
  danger signs. Some programmes want every infant seen. The baby's age is
  not given; zinc is not mentioned.
- Only the sachet is taught for oral rehydration, not the sugar-salt recipe.
- Food groups are called قوت، نمو، محافظت (strength, growth, protection).
- "Keep a way for fresh air where a fire burns" (see also poisoning below).

## Health 3: first aid

This pack follows standard first-aid guidance as the AI understands it. The
facts it was written from, every simplification and every review change are
in [`content/garden/3/SOURCES.md`](../content/garden/3/SOURCES.md). A
qualified person must sign it off before any child sees it. Open decisions:

- **Burns:** "has blisters → clinic" is stricter than usual and sends most
  scalds to a distant clinic. Missing: clothes on fire, burns around a limb,
  smoke or flame to the face.
- **Bleeding:** raising the limb is taught although it is no longer in the
  guidelines (harmless). Avoiding contact with someone else's blood is not
  taught.
- **The "help at home" basket** (a small clean cut that stopped, a small
  burn without a blister, a nosebleed that stopped) was derived, not taken
  from a source.
- **Chest compressions:** should children of 8 to 14 be taught them at all,
  or only the sequence up to "bring an adult"? Only an adult casualty is
  shown; nothing is said for a child or after drowning. "Do not stop until
  help takes over" has no realistic end where no ambulance exists.
- **Choking:** back blows by "an older child" has no age. Abdominal thrusts
  are left to adults and not described.
- **Bites:** is rabies vaccine actually available at the local clinic? Where
  do families go if not? The snake-bite advice (keep still; do not cut, suck
  or tie) was added in review.
- **Poisoning:**
  - "Nothing to eat or drink" is the rule taught. For a swallowed cleaning
    liquid some manuals allow sips of water. Keep one rule or two?
  - Everyone who felt unwell from smoke "is seen at the clinic". Workable at
    night, hours away?
  - How strongly to warn against a charcoal brazier indoors where it is the
    family's only heat.
  - Not covered: pesticide breathed in or sprayed on someone in the field,
    fits after poisoning, unknown plants and mushrooms, medicines taken by
    mistake.
- **Words:** «مواد غایطه» (stool), «ادرار» (urine), «تیل خاک» (kerosene),
  «منقل», «زهر کشت» (field poison), «آب سرد» (said for cool water).

## Optional pack: My rights, your rights

Read [`content/garden/4-rights/SOURCES.md`](../content/garden/4-rights/SOURCES.md)
first. **The local team decides, class by class, whether this pack is
installed at all.** If the word «حق» itself is the problem, leave the pack
off the card; do not edit it. Sentences a cautious reader may question:

- "Every child, girl and boy, has the right to learn." (said twice)
- "Whatever language they speak at home", "rich or poor".
- "Nobody may hurt a child", which can be heard as a comment on how adults
  correct children.
- "Children must not do work that is dangerous, or work that does not let
  them learn", and the story in which children tell their father the work is
  too much and he changes it.
- "Something that frightens you is not a secret to keep. Tell an adult you
  trust", and naming the teacher and the health worker as such adults.
- Pictures with girls and boys together.

## Optional pack: The world, Switzerland and Basel

Read [`content/garden/4-world/SOURCES.md`](../content/garden/4-world/SOURCES.md).
It is optional because it tells where the device comes from, which the local
team may or may not want.

- Words to settle: قاره or براعظم; بحر آرام or بحرالکاهل; انترکتیکا;
  اقیانوسیه; «یخمالک چوبی» for sledge.
- "Grüezi" is the formal greeting; children in Basel say "Sali" or "Hoi".
  Someone from Basel should choose.
- "In winter there is much snow" is true of the mountains, less of Basel.
- Maps show no country borders except the countries a lesson is about.
  Outlines are from Natural Earth and follow its view of the world.
- The festival scene shows drums and painted lanterns only.
