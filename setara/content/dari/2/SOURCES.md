# Dari 2: The whole alphabet (`fa-2`), version 2

Written by an AI (Claude). **No Dari speaker, teacher or child has seen or
heard any of it.** Version 1 had no SOURCES file; its open points are in
`docs/review-notes.md` ("Dari reading 1 and 2") and still stand.

## What it teaches

Curriculum, level 2: know all 32 letters by sound and name, in every position
in a word; read short vowels. The twenty letters that `fa-1` did not teach are
introduced two by two; the twelve known ones are reviewed.

## What version 2 changed, and why

The owner's words: "More and more varied content. All letters. Not always the
same questions." Measured on version 1: 104 of 302 spoken questions occurred
more than twice in the pack, six letters were the right answer four or five
times in Alefba Garden, and a replayed round was the same round.

- Every game except the stories is a **pool** (`"draw"`).
- **Game ids, letters and all 42 old rounds are kept**; old rows are still
  there, some reworded, some turned round (letter shown, picture asked).
- 26 new rounds, three new games.

## Decisions

- **Wording and answers** as in `fa-1` (see its SOURCES.md): no spoken
  question more than twice in the pack (version 1: 104 of 302); one answer is
  right four times (غ in Alefba Garden), all others three at most.
- **One sound, several letters.** A first-letter question never offers ص for
  س, ط for ت, ح for ه or the like, because by ear both would be right. The
  journey teaches the fact itself. The stand-in voice also merges ق and غ, so
  those two are kept apart in sound questions as well.
- **ح and ه** are both spoken "he": never among the choices of one question,
  and their key word is said with the name where it matters.
- **ذ** now has a picture word, ذره‌بین (magnifying glass); the spoken key
  word of version 1, ذره, stays in its old row.
- **Short vowels**: wrong choices are the same word with the other two marks,
  as in version 1; some are real words (دَم، دُر، نَه).
- **Voice**: as in `fa-1`.
