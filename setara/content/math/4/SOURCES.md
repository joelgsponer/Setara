# Math 4: Times Tables and Sharing (unreviewed draft)

Generated by `content/math/generate.py`; do not edit the CSV files, change the
script and run it. The pictures of this level and of level 5 (fraction circles,
thirds, charts) are drawn by `content/math/draw_pictures.py`.

## What it teaches (spec, section 8, level 4)

All tables to 10, division as sharing, halves, quarters and thirds; weight and
volume, perimeter, calendar. 11 games, 63 rounds, 1084 items (version 1: 9
games, 45 rounds, 321 items).

## Pools (version 2)

Every game has `"draw": 8` (Twin Tiles: 6 pairs). A round is a pool of 16 to
24 questions and a play asks eight of them, a different eight each time. The
generator makes each pool with a fixed seed per round, spreads the answers
evenly over the round's number range and allows no answer to be right more
than three times in a pool. Where a round has only a handful of possible
answers at all (seven days, the four signs) they are balanced instead; in a
sorter every basket gets at least half its even share.
`python3 content/math/generate.py --check` checks all of this again from the
finished rows and prints the table.

Smaller pools: Share the Bread 03 (14 sums: these answers have no more in the
tables), Twin Tiles 03 (10: the tables give only ten different quotients).

## Decisions taken

- **Words.** The operation words of levels 1 to 3 are kept (جمع، منفی، ضرب،
  مساوی); division is «تقسیم» in the same pattern: «دوازده تقسیم سه چند
  می‌شود؟».
- **Fractions.** نیم، چارک، سه چارک for 1/2, 1/4, 3/4; thirds are «یک بر سه» and
  «دو بر سه» (the school reading of a written fraction), introduced as «یک حصه
  از سه حصه برابر». No written fraction appears before level 5.
- **Sums are written left to right in Western digits only** (`3×4`, `12÷3`).
  Inside right-to-left text the builder would draw a Dari-digit sum in the
  wrong order. Single numbers appear in both digit systems (Number Hop round
  7, one row in each table round of Carpet Weaver, half of Number Rain, some
  halves).
- **Number line.** The journey engine only draws hops for + and − within 20,
  so the multiplication and division rounds of Number Hop have no number line.
- **Weight and volume are metric** (کیلو، گرام، لیتر). «چارک» is used only for
  a quarter of a number or a thing, never for a weight, because چارک is also a
  traditional weight; quarter kilos are therefore left out (half, one and a
  half, two and a half kilos are used).
- **Perimeter** is «محیط», explained in the round's instruction as «دور شکل»;
  the life problems say «دور». Sides are «طول» and «عرض».
- **Calendar.** Week from شنبه to جمعه; the twelve solar months by their
  Afghan names (حمل … حوت); seasons بهار، تابستان، خزان، زمستان, three months
  each. The day and month names are written words, so the game has
  `read_choices`: every choice is read aloud.
- **Legibility.** Where a written Dari label would have been smaller than 30 px
  (یک بر سه on a basket or a pair card), a picture basket or a speaker card is
  used instead.
- Distractors are computed from typical mistakes: the neighbouring table fact,
  adding instead of multiplying, the divisor instead of the quotient, half
  instead of a quarter, area or half the perimeter instead of the perimeter,
  and one more or less. The script asserts that no two choices on screen have
  the same value and checks every answer a second time from the written row.

## For the human reviewer

A Dari-speaking mathematics teacher should decide:

1. Is «تقسیم» right as it is said here («دوازده تقسیم سه»), or «تقسیم بر»?
2. Thirds: «یک بر سه» / «دو بر سه», or «سه‌یک», «ثلث», «یک سوم»? And is «سه
   چارک» the natural word for three quarters?
3. «حصه» for a part of a whole, «نفر» for the people who share.
4. «محیط» and «دور»; «طول» and «عرض» (or «دراز» and «بر»).
5. «کیلو» (not «کیلوگرام»), «گرام», «لیتر», «تانکی», «سطل», «ظرف». Should the
   traditional weights (سیر، چارک، پاو) be taught beside the metric ones?
6. Calendar: is a week that starts on شنبه and the plain statement «روز آخر
   هفته جمعه است» wanted as written? Spelling of the day names (سه‌شنبه,
   پنجشنبه) and of the months. No holiday or festival is named on purpose.
7. «یک شبانه‌روز بیست و چهار ساعت دارد».
8. Prices are numbers chosen for the arithmetic, not real prices (as in
   levels 2 and 3). «مشتری … می‌خرد» / «… می‌خری» wording of the bills.
9. Word problems: «قطار چوکی», «صندوق انار», «بوری آرد», «خانواده». The line
   «هر گوسفند چهار پای دارد».
10. The picture `bread` (whole naan) is an oval loaf while `bread_half`,
    `bread_third` and `bread_quarter` are cut from a round one. The whole
    should probably be redrawn round; it is an older library picture and was
    not touched.

11. New in version 2: «دو چند» / «سه چند» for double and triple; «جای خالی»
    for a gap; «کدام نشان درست است؟» with the choices +, −, ×, ÷; «درست» /
    «غلط»; «عددهایی که از ضرب یک عدد در خودش ساخته می‌شوند»; «هم در جدول ضرب
    دو و هم در جدول ضرب سه». The story sentences are in `STORIES` in the
    generator («رمه», «قطارهای شش تایی», «خریده می‌توانی»).
12. `orange_half`, `orange_third`, `orange_quarter` and the same of a
    pomegranate are new pictures, drawn by the script like the bread pieces.
    The library's whole pomegranate shows a cut piece beside the fruit and is
    therefore not used as a "whole".

Nobody has listened to the audio, and nothing has been tried on a handheld or
with children.
