the round, no answer right more than three times in a pool, `python3
content/math/generate.py --check` checks the finished rows again. Running the

## Measuring a dose: what was done to keep it safe

The spec asks for "measuring a medicine dose". The pack teaches the
measuring and the counting only:

- No medicine is named. No amount is tied to an age, a weight or an illness.
  The word is always the plain «دوا».
- Every such question says where the amount comes from: «کارمند صحی گفته
  است …» (the health worker said) or «روی بوتل دوا نوشته است …» (the writing
  on the bottle says). The help of every such question ends with «اندازه دوا
  را همیشه کارمند صحی یا نوشته روی بوتل می‌گوید، نه حساب ما.» The generator
  asserts both for every row that contains the word «دوا» (`check_answers6`).
- The amounts change from question to question (5, 10, 15, 20 millilitres;
  bottles of 60 to 200), so that no number can be taken home as "the dose".
- The spoon is always «قاشق اندازه‌گیری» (a measuring spoon of 5
  millilitres), never a kitchen spoon.
- It is 14 of 1077 questions (Shape Mason round 5: 8 of 18, round 7: 6 of
  18); the rest of the four measuring rounds is oil, milk, water, tea, lemon
  juice. The marked cup never holds medicine.

A health professional must still decide whether these 14 questions stay. If
not, delete the lists `med`, `day` and `lasts` in `mason6` and raise the
numbers of the cooking questions in the two `pool6` calls: nothing else
depends on them.

## Decisions taken

- **Percent** is «فیصد», written `25%` in Western digits (as fractions and
  decimals are since level 5). It is introduced as "out of a hundred" on a
  hundred square, then tied to the fractions and decimals of level 5
  (`25% = 25/100 = 1/4 = 0.25`; 25 pul of an afghani). Only percents that
  are easy by ear are worked out of an amount: 10 (divide by ten), 20, 25,
  50, 75, 100 and multiples of ten. The help always says the way: "25
  percent means a quarter. 80 divided by 4 equals 20."
- **Decimals of a percent** are written with two places (`0.20`, `0.05`), so
  that "twenty out of a hundred" can be seen in them. Number Rain also uses
  the short form (`0.2`).
- **Ratio** is «نسبت» and is read «دو به سه», because «دو بر سه» is already
  the fraction 2/3. It is written `2:3`. Sharing in a ratio is always
  explained as "add the parts, find one part, multiply".
- **The average** is «اوسط» and is explained as sharing out evenly («اگر همه
  را یکجا کنیم و برابر تقسیم کنیم …»). All averages are whole numbers of
  three to five small numbers.
- **Profit and loss** are «فایده» and «ضرر», buying and selling price «قیمت
  خرید» and «قیمت فروش». Profit as a percent of the buying price is not
  taught.
- **Budget**: income «عاید», spending «مصرف», saving «پس‌انداز». No debt, no
  credit and no interest appear anywhere.
- **Scale** is «مقیاس»; a map is «نقشه». The maps are invented: three places
  (house, school, well, shop, tree, bridge, clinic) on two straight roads,
  no borders, no real place. A picture may not carry digits, so a road is
  drawn as pieces coloured in turn like a scale bar, and the question says
  "each piece of road on the map is one centimetre". On the screen a piece
  is of course not one centimetre long.
- **Measures**: «ملی‌لیتر», «لیتر», «کیلومتر», «سانتی‌متر».
- **People**: no names. Girls and boys, women and men take turns as the one
  who saves, sells, reads, walks or is counted (the class is counted once by
  its girls and once by its boys).
- **Both digit systems**: Dari digits in a part of Count the Flock 05, Number
  Hop 07 and Shape Mason 01. Percents, ratios, fractions and decimals are
  Western only.
- `val()` in the generator now also reads a percent, a ratio and a hundred
  square, so the existing checks (never two choices of the same value, one
  right answer only, pairs with one partner) cover the new forms.

## For the human reviewer

A Dari-speaking mathematics teacher should decide:

1. «فیصد» (not «درصد»); the sign `%` after Western digits; is the Dari-digit
   form «۲۵٪» wanted as well?
2. «نسبت … به …» for a ratio and the colon `2:3`. Afghan school books may
   read a ratio «دو بر سه» like a fraction; the pack keeps the two apart.
3. «اوسط» for the mean (not «میانگین»); «اوسط هر روز چند تخم دادند؟».
4. «فایده» / «ضرر» (or «مفاد» / «تاوان»)? «قیمت خرید», «قیمت فروش», «تخفیف».
5. «عاید» / «مصرف» / «پس‌انداز»; «کیسه پس‌انداز» for the purse.
6. «مقیاس»; «هر تکه راه روی نقشه یک سانتی‌متر است»; «دریا» for a river.
7. «قاشق اندازه‌گیری», «پیاله اندازه‌گیری», «هر خط … ملی‌لیتر است».
8. «دو چند، سه چند … شش چند» for two to six times as much.
9. The recipes are numbers chosen for the arithmetic (rice to water 2 to 3,
   milk tea 1 to 2, dough 3 to 1, doogh 1 to 2, sand to cement 3 to 1, earth
   to straw 4 to 1, barley to straw 1 to 3). Are any of them so wrong that
   they would be laughed at? «مصاله دیوار» for mortar, «کاه‌گل».
10. Prices, incomes and savings are invented (a blanket 400 to 900 afghani,
    a goat 3000 to 6000, a family's month 3000 to 9000). Are the sizes
    sensible? Things a family saves for: a goat, a sewing machine, a
    bicycle, school books.
11. The arrow in «50 → 70» (bought for 50, sold for 70) on a sorter card.
12. True or False round 3 shows only the numbers (`3, 6, 6, 5`); the claim
    about their average is spoken. Is that enough?
13. The hundred squares, the maps and the cup are small on the 3.5 inch
    screen (about 2 cm); 1 percent and 99 percent need a careful look. They
    must be tried with children on the device.
14. By ear and with four choices: 75 percent of 380, a discount of 25
    percent on 1900, an average of five numbers. Too hard without paper?
    The guide tells the teacher to let pupils write.

A health professional should decide:

15. Whether the 14 dose questions stay (see above), and the sentence
    «اندازه دوا را همیشه کارمند صحی یا نوشته روی بوتل می‌گوید، نه حساب ما.»
