# Math 5: Large Numbers and Fractions (unreviewed draft)

Generated by `content/math/generate.py`; do not edit the CSV files, change the
script and run it. The fraction circles and the charts are drawn by
`content/math/draw_pictures.py` (the charts from the same list of numbers the
questions are made from).

## What it teaches (spec, section 8, level 5)

Large numbers, multi-digit multiplication, comparing and adding fractions,
decimals through money; area, reading tables and simple charts. 10 games, 47
rounds, 335 items.

## Decisions taken

- **Number words.** Afghan forms continue: «یک هزار و پنج‌صد», «ده هزار», «صد
  هزار», «یک میلیون»; 1000 alone stays «هزار» as in level 3.
- **Fractions** are read «دو بر پنج»; numerator «صورت», denominator «مخرج».
  They are written `2/5` in Western digits only: between Dari digits the slash
  is read as a decimal mark, and the builder cannot stack a fraction.
- **Decimals** are read «دو اعشاریه پنجاه» and written with a point (`2.50`),
  in Western digits only. They are introduced through money: 1 afghani = 100
  pul.
- **Adding fractions** keeps to one denominator, and the sums are chosen so
  that the result needs no simplifying. The typical mistake (adding the
  denominators too, 3/10 for 1/5 + 2/5) is always among the wrong choices.
- **Area** is «مساحت» in «متر مربع». The counting round uses the engine's own
  counted pictures, which lie in full rows only for 4, 6, 9, 12, 16 and 20
  squares; that is why it has six items.
- **Charts.** A picture has no text or digits, so a chart is three columns of
  blocks over three small pictures, and a table three rows of dots read from
  the right. Three things, not four, so that the small pictures stay
  recognisable in the 140 px picture box. "Which is the fewest?" is not asked:
  the builder always deals four choices, the fourth is a thing that is not in
  the chart, and it could be argued to be the fewest. The question "of which
  thing are there two?" takes its place.
- **Legibility.** The 1000 note is only offered with four kinds of note so
  that its number is drawn at 24 px; Number Rain uses no number wider than
  three digits; baskets carry one large word (کمتر، نیم، بیشتر) and the
  comparison is spoken.
- **Both digit systems**: Dari digits in Count the Flock round 4, one row of
  Carpet Weaver, two rules of Number Rain, half of the area counting round.
- The script asserts that no two choices on screen have the same value (2.5
  and 2.50, 1/2 and 2/4) and checks every answer a second time from the
  written row.

## For the human reviewer

A Dari-speaking mathematics teacher should decide:

1. «میلیون» or «ملیون»; «یک هزار و …» or «هزار و …» for 1001 to 1999; «صد
   هزار» for 100,000.
2. How large numbers should be grouped on a card. They are written without a
   separator (`250000`); a seven-digit number is the widest that fits.
3. Fractions read «دو بر پنج»; «صورت» and «مخرج»; «کسر». Should the Dari-digit
   form of a written fraction be taught, and how is it written in the school
   books?
4. Decimals: «اعشاریه» and the point (or «٫» / a comma?); reading 2.05 as «دو
   اعشاریه صفر پنج» and 2.50 as «دو اعشاریه پنجاه».
5. Money: is «پول» (100 to the afghani) a sensible way to meet decimals when
   pul coins are not in use? An alternative is metres and centimetres.
6. «مساحت», «متر مربع», «طول», «عرض», «محیط»; «فرش» for the floor of squares.
7. «گراف» for a chart and «جدول» for a table; «خانه‌های … را بشمار».
8. «بر ده تقسیم می‌شوند» for divisible by ten; «جدول ضرب دوازده / بیست و پنج».
9. Prices are numbers chosen for the arithmetic, not real prices. «امروز به
   نیم قیمت است»; «حصه یک نفر را بده».
10. Word problems name a school with several classes, a garden with rows of
    trees, sacks of 7, 25 or 50 kilos: are the sizes sensible?
11. Is two digits times two digits (Number Hop round 6, by ear, four choices)
    too hard without paper? It may be better as a teacher-led round.
12. The charts are small on the 3.5 inch screen (about 15 mm). They read well
    on a PC screenshot; they must be tried with children on the device.

Nobody has listened to the audio, and nothing has been tried on a handheld or
with children.
