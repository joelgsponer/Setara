## Pools (version 2)

Every game has `"draw": 8` (Twin Tiles: 6 pairs). A round is a pool of 16 to
24 questions and a play asks eight of them, a different eight each time. The
generator makes each pool with a fixed seed per round, spreads the answers
evenly over the round's number range and allows no answer to be right more
than three times in a pool. Where a round has only a handful of possible
answers at all (four shapes, twelve hours, the four signs) they are balanced
instead; in a sorter every basket gets at least half its even share. Pools
below 16 exist only where the material is limited; they are listed below.
`python3 content/math/generate.py --check` checks all of this again from the
finished rows and prints the table.

Smaller pool: Share the Bread 03 (11): five things are drawn whole and six in
halves.

## Decisions taken

- **What makes ten** (Number Hop 02) was "3 + 7 = ?", always ten. It is now
  the missing number («سه جمع چند، ده می‌شود؟», shown as `3+_=10`) and ten
  minus a number, so the answers run from 1 to 9.
- **Coins** are written as sums (`10+10+5`), the wrong choices are sets with
  a total close by. Three coins at most: a choice card holds eight signs.
- **Halves** are explained by doubling («نیم چهارده، هفت است، چون هفت جمع
  هفت مساوی چهارده»), not by division, which comes in level 4.
- **Clock stories**: school, baking, the shepherd, a walk; always whole hours,
  the answer is a clock.
- The whole pomegranate of the library shows a cut piece beside the fruit, so
  it is not used as a "whole"; its half and quarter are.
- Market Scales round 1 keeps the coins 1, 2, 5 and never asks for 13
  (`tests/market.lua` pays 13 there and expects it to be wrong).
