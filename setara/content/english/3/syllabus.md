# English 3 "My day": syllabus

20 units = 20 classroom lessons. Text is exactly as in `items.csv` (`en` = what the device says, Dari = the meaning on the help button).
In dialogues `f:` is the woman's voice, `m:` the man's voice, `//` separates the speakers.
"Review" lists earlier words that come back in the unit's picture game; they are not new.
Level 3 builds on levels 1 and 2 (see `../1/syllabus.md`, `../2/syllabus.md`): I am, This is, I have, I like, Do you like ...?, numbers 1-20, plurals, first-letter sounds.
New language: present simple for the daily routine (01-03, 07), numbers 21-100 (04-05), What time is it? It is ... o'clock, at ... o'clock (06-07), the days of the week, today, tomorrow, on Friday (08-09), I don't ... (09), I wear, put on, take off (11-12), he/she + verb-s, we, they (13), Do you ...? Does she ...? Yes, she does. No, he doesn't. always, never (14), Where is ...? in, on, under, next to (15-17), reading three-letter words (18-19).
The week is taught from Saturday to Friday, as the school week runs. Only full hours are taught; half past and quarter hours are not in this pack.
Numbers, the days, do/does and reading are practised mainly in the game sheets (`numbers.csv`, `shopkeeper.csv`, `day_journey.csv`, `yes_no.csv`, `baskets.csv`, `sound_catcher.csv`, `memory.csv`, `sentence_train.csv`), because they have no pictures of their own.
