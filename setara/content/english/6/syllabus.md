# English 6: The wider world: syllabus

20 units = 20 classroom lessons. Generated from the sheets when the pack was written; if a sheet changes, change this file with it.
`en` is what the device says, Dari is the meaning on the help button. In dialogues `f:` is the woman's voice, `m:` the man's, `//` separates the speakers.
Every unit is a teaching sequence, played in the order of the round letters: PRESENT (a Word Stall, b Echo), UNDERSTAND (c), PRACTISE (three different games from d to l), USE (m). Review units 10 and 20 only practise and use.

## 01 What does he do? (او چه کار می‌کند؟)

- Pattern: What does he do? He is a farmer. She is a baker. What is her job?
- Can do: name common jobs and say what job a person has
- Words with a picture: farmer = دهقان; shepherd = چوپان; baker = نانوا; driver = دریور; builder = گلکار; carpenter = نجار
- Words, sentences and dialogues (Echo):
  - job = کار
  - What does he do? = او چه کار می‌کند؟
  - He is a farmer. = او دهقان است.
  - f: What is your father's job? // m: He is a carpenter. = کار پدرت چیست؟ او نجار است.
  - m: What does your mother do? // f: She is a baker. = مادرت چه کار می‌کند؟ او نانوا است.
- Games, in order:
  - `en-6-01-a` Word Stall (listen_pick): PRESENT: hear each new word, pick its picture (Dari meaning on the help button)
  - `en-6-01-b` Echo (say_it): PRESENT: hear, say aloud and compare every word, sentence and dialogue of the unit
  - `en-6-01-c` Listen In (story): UNDERSTAND: a short dialogue that uses the new language, explained in Dari first, with two questions
  - `en-6-01-d` Riddles (listen_pick): PRACTISE: a spoken riddle, the word itself is not said
  - `en-6-01-e` Yes or No? (listen_pick): PRACTISE: a picture and a yes/no question
  - `en-6-01-l` Pairs (pairs): PRACTISE: find the two cards that belong together (reading)
  - `en-6-01-m` Read and Decide (story): USE: a reading text with questions asking what and why
