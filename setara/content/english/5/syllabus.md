# English 5: Health and help: syllabus

20 units = 20 classroom lessons. Generated from the sheets when the pack was written; if a sheet changes, change this file with it.
`en` is what the device says, Dari is the meaning on the help button. In dialogues `f:` is the woman's voice, `m:` the man's, `//` separates the speakers.
Every unit is a teaching sequence, played in the order of the round letters: PRESENT (a Word Stall, b Echo), UNDERSTAND (c), PRACTISE (three different games from d to l), USE (m). Review units 10 and 20 only practise and use.

## 01 How do you feel? (چه حال داری؟)

- Pattern: How do you feel? I feel sick. I am not well. Are you hungry? No, I am thirsty.
- Can do: say how she feels and ask someone else
- Words with a picture: sick = مریض; tired = مانده; hungry = گرسنه; thirsty = تشنه; hot = گرم; cold = خنک
- Words, sentences and dialogues (Echo):
  - How do you feel? = چه حال داری؟
  - I feel sick. = من خود را مریض احساس می‌کنم.
  - I am not well. = من جور نیستم.
  - f: How are you today? // m: I am not well. I feel hot. = امروز چطور هستی؟ جور نیستم. گرمی می‌کنم.
  - m: Are you hungry? // f: No, I am thirsty. = گرسنه هستی؟ نه، تشنه هستم.
- Games, in order:
  - `en-5-01-a` Word Stall (listen_pick): PRESENT: hear each new word, pick its picture (Dari meaning on the help button)
  - `en-5-01-b` Echo (say_it): PRESENT: hear, say aloud and compare every word, sentence and dialogue of the unit
  - `en-5-01-c` Listen In (story): UNDERSTAND: a short dialogue that uses the new language, explained in Dari first, with two questions
  - `en-5-01-d` Riddles (listen_pick): PRACTISE: a spoken riddle, the word itself is not said
  - `en-5-01-e` Yes or No? (listen_pick): PRACTISE: a picture and a yes/no question
  - `en-5-01-i` Sentence Train (builder): PRACTISE: build the sentence from word tiles
  - `en-5-01-m` Read and Decide (story): USE: a reading text with questions asking what and why
