# English 4 "At the bazaar": syllabus

20 units = 20 classroom lessons. Text is exactly as in `items.csv` (`en` = what the device says, Dari = the meaning on the help button).
In dialogues `f:` is the woman's voice, `m:` the man's voice, `//` separates the speakers.
"Review" lists earlier words that come back in the unit's picture game; they are not new.
Level 4 builds on levels 1 to 3: greetings, I am, This is, I have, I like, plurals, Give me ..., please, numbers to 100, present simple with do / does, home and clothes.
New language: I want to buy / He sells (02), How much is it? It is ... afghani (03), How much are ...? They are ... (05), a kilo of, half a kilo of (07), How many ... do you want? / How much ... do you want? (08), a bottle / packet / jar of (09), I need (11), some and any (12), can and can't, Can I help you? (13), Can I have ..., please? (14), Excuse me, where ...? You're welcome. Anything else? (17), too expensive, cheaper, all together, hundreds (18), open and closed (19).

## 01 The bazaar

New words:
- bazaar = بازار
- shop = دکان
- shopkeeper = دکاندار
- customer = مشتری
- street = سرک
- cart = کراچی

Phrases and sentences:
- This is the bazaar. = این بازار است.
- Let's go to the bazaar. = بیا که به بازار برویم.
- f: Hello! // m: Hello! Welcome to my shop. = سلام! سلام! به دکان من خوش آمدید.
- m: Where is the shopkeeper? // f: He is in the shop. = دکاندار کجاست؟ او در دکان است.
