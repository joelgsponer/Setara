# English 2 "My world": syllabus

20 units = 20 classroom lessons. Text is exactly as in `items.csv` (`en` = what the device says, Dari = the meaning on the help button).
In dialogues `f:` is the woman's voice, `m:` the man's voice, `//` separates the speakers.
"Review" lists earlier words that come back in the unit's picture game; they are not new.
Level 2 builds on level 1 (see `../1/syllabus.md`): greetings, I am, This is, What is this?, numbers 1-10, colours, big and small, Give me ..., please.
New language: This is my ... / Who is this? (01-02), I have (03, 05), Touch your ... (04), numbers 11-20 (06-07), How many ...? (08), I like (09, 11), I don't like (12), Do you like ...? Yes, I do. / No, I don't. (13), plurals with -s (08, 16).
Plurals that do not simply add -s are taught as whole phrases: two feet (05), four sheep (16), potatoes and tomatoes (12).

## 01 My family

New words:
- mother = مادر
- father = پدر
- sister = خواهر
- brother = برادر
- family = فامیل

Phrases and sentences:
- This is my mother. = این مادر من است.
- This is my father. = این پدر من است.
- Who is this? = این کیست؟
- f: Who is this? // m: This is my sister. = این کیست؟ این خواهر من است.
- m: Who is this? // f: This is my brother. = این کیست؟ این برادر من است.
