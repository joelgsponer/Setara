# English 1 "Hello": syllabus

20 units = 20 classroom lessons. Text is exactly as in `items.csv` (`en` = what the device says, Dari = the meaning on the help button).
In dialogues `f:` is the woman's voice, `m:` the man's voice, `//` separates the speakers.
"Review" lists earlier words that come back in the unit's picture game; they are not new.
The 15 classroom commands: Stand up, Sit down, Clap, Listen, Look (02); Open, Close, Come here, Go, Point to (10); Draw, Write, Read, Count, Say (15).

## 01 Hello!

New words:
- Hello = سلام
- Goodbye = خداحافظ
- Good morning = صبح بخیر
- teacher = معلم
- class = صنف

Phrases and sentences:
- Hello, teacher! = سلام معلم
- f: Good morning, class! // m: Good morning, teacher! = صبح بخیر صنف. صبح بخیر معلم.
- f: Goodbye, class! // m: Goodbye, teacher! = خداحافظ صنف. خداحافظ معلم.
