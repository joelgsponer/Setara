# Pictures used by English level 1. Format: name: what to draw.
# Style (docs/authoring.md): thick dark outline, flat colours, no text, readable at 3 cm.
# People: rural Afghan children about 8 years old, modest everyday dress; girls with a headscarf, boys in perahan tunban (long shirt), with or without a cap.
# Girls and boys alternate through the list on purpose. A local reviewer approves every picture of a person.

# Unit 01
wave: a girl facing us, smiling, one hand raised and waving hello
goodbye: a boy seen walking away through a doorway with his school bag, turning his head back and waving
sun: a yellow morning sun rising over two low hills
teacher: a woman teacher standing next to a blackboard, holding a piece of chalk (modest long dress, headscarf)
class: a group of five small children sitting in two rows on the floor, seen from the front, some girls and some boys (the local reviewer may ask for a girls-only and a boys-only version)

# Unit 02 commands (one child doing the action)
stand_up: a girl standing up straight in front of a low bench, with an arrow pointing up beside her
sit_down: a boy sitting cross-legged on a floor mat, with an arrow pointing down beside him
clap: a girl clapping her hands, small motion lines around the hands
listen: a boy holding one hand behind his ear, small sound waves coming towards the ear
look: a girl shading her eyes with one hand and looking to the side, a dotted line going from her eyes

# Unit 03
me: a girl pointing at her own chest with her hand (meaning "I")
you: a boy pointing straight out of the picture at the viewer (meaning "you")

# Unit 04
boy: one boy standing, facing us, smiling, hands at his sides
girl: one girl standing, facing us, smiling, hands at her sides
yes: a large green tick mark
no: a large red cross mark (an X)

# Unit 08 (plain versions: keep these colours so that the coloured versions below look different)
book: one closed book with a brown cover
pen: one black ballpoint pen, lying diagonally
pencil: one yellow pencil with a sharp point, lying diagonally
bag: one tan cloth school bag with a shoulder strap

# Unit 09
chair: a simple wooden chair
table: a simple wooden table
door: a closed wooden door in its frame
window: a window with four panes in a mud-brick wall
board: a black classroom board on a wall, empty, with a piece of chalk on its ledge

# Unit 10 commands
open: a boy pulling a door open; the door is half open and a curved arrow shows it swinging open
close: a girl pushing a door shut; the door is almost closed and a curved arrow shows it swinging shut
come_here: a girl beckoning with her whole hand towards herself, an arrow pointing towards her
go: a boy walking away from us, an arrow on the ground pointing ahead of him
point: a girl pointing with her arm stretched out at a board on the wall

# Units 11 and 12 (a round paint blob in the colour, same shape for all eight)

# Unit 13

bread: one long flat Afghan naan bread
water: a clear glass full of water

# Unit 14
friends: two girls standing side by side, smiling, one with her arm around the other's shoulder
he: a boy standing on the right; from the left edge a hand and arm point at him
she: a girl standing on the right; from the left edge a hand and arm point at her

# Unit 15 commands
draw: a girl drawing a flower on a sheet of paper with a pencil; the flower is clearly visible
write: a boy writing in a notebook with a pen; the page shows wavy lines of writing (no real letters)
read: a girl holding an open book up in both hands and looking into it
count: a boy counting on his fingers, touching the fingers of one hand with the other hand
say: a girl speaking with her mouth open, sound waves coming out of her mouth (no speech bubble text)

# Unit 16

# Unit 17

# Unit 18

sorry: a boy looking sorry, hand on his head, beside a glass of water he has knocked over
give: two girls; one hands a pen to the other, an arrow shows the pen moving to the second girl

# Unit 19

# Story scenes (4:3, full background)
scene_sara_hello: Sara, a girl of eight with a headscarf, stands in front of her mud-brick house in the morning and waves
scene_sara_bag: Sara holds up her green cloth school bag with both hands to show it; the bag is clearly green
scene_sara_apple: Sara takes a big red apple out of her green bag and holds it up
scene_laila_sad: Laila, another girl with a headscarf, sits on a step outside the school with a sad face; Sara walks up to her with her green bag
scene_sara_gives_apple: Sara hands the red apple to Laila; Laila starts to smile
scene_girls_happy: Sara and Laila sit side by side, both smiling; Laila holds the apple; Sara waves goodbye to us
scene_omar_hello: Omar, a boy of nine in perahan tunban, stands at the door of the village school and waves
scene_omar_classroom: Omar inside the empty classroom; the door, the black board, a window and a table are all clearly visible and apart from each other
scene_omar_ali: Omar stands beside his friend Ali, a slightly smaller boy; Omar's hand is on Ali's shoulder
scene_three_pens: a table top with exactly three pens in a row: one red, one blue, one black; the two boys look at them
scene_omar_gives_pen: Omar hands the red pen to Ali; both smile
scene_boys_goodbye: Omar and Ali stand at the classroom door, smiling and waving goodbye to us
