# garden-2 (Health and environment 2: My home), version 2

**Written by an AI (Claude). Not reviewed by a Dari speaker, a teacher or a
health professional; built with silent speech only, so nothing has been
heard.** `docs/review-notes.md` ("Health 1 and 2") holds the open health
points (tap and pump water sorted as clean, boiling without a time, the
diarrhoea story, only the sachet for oral rehydration, the names of the food
groups, "a way for fresh air where a fire burns"). Version 2 leaves every one
of them exactly as it was.

## What version 2 changes (16 November 2026)

Version 1 had 20 rounds that were the same at every play. Version 2 has 36
rounds, and every round except the stories is a pool.

- **Existing games keep their ids, letters, rounds and every old row.** New
  rows were appended: sorters and picking games hold 10 to 14 items per round
  and ask 8; `clean_hands` has one more sequence per round and asks 3.
- **New forms inside the old picking games:** riddles, and questions that
  name what is wrong ("Which deed makes the stream dirty?").
- **Six new games:** `why_stories` (story, 5: where rain comes from, why a
  tree holds the soil, where rubbish goes, the journey of a fly, the three
  food groups), `what_next` (builder, 2), `odd_one_out` (listen_pick, 2),
  `true_or_not` (sorter with tick and cross, 2), `go_together` (pairs, 3:
  where food comes from; problem and answer; trees, water, mountains),
  `quick_hands` (catch, 2).

## Health content: no new advice

No dose, medicine or remedy was added. The new rows restate rules of version
1 in its wording, or state their plain opposite as a false sentence in
`true_or_not` ("We go to the latrine barefoot": false). What was reused:
boil, cover, clean ladle, clean glass, do not dip a hand, do not mix
unboiled water in, filter muddy water through a clean cloth and then boil;
latrine always, covered, far from the well, sandals, soap afterwards, kept
clean with broom and water; flies carry germs from dirt to food, cover the
food; dry wood smokes less, open window and door, chimney, never burn
plastic; ORS packet from the clinic, prepared by an adult, whole packet,
stir, small sips often; a sick child still needs food; danger sign, quickly
to the clinic; three food groups with their version 1 members; sweets, fizzy
drinks and sugar only a little.

Points where a new row goes a small step beyond the old text, for a health
professional to confirm:
- **Foods newly placed in a group:** onion, cucumber, cabbage, melon, orange,
  aubergine, peach (protection); cheese (growth, next to milk and yoghurt);
  flour and wheat (strength). Sweet biscuits and chocolate join "only a
  little"; carrot, grapes and pomegranate join "every day".
- **Water sorter round 01** gained six items, none of them tap or pump water:
  boiled water in a clean glass and boiled water taken with a clean ladle
  (clean); muddy water, stream water with rubbish, water animals stand in,
  open-well water (unclean). The open point about tap and pump stays.
- "We fetch water in a clean bucket" (good) / "in a dirty bucket" (danger);
  "a plastic bag in the stream" makes water dirty.
- The fly story says germs "stick to the fly's legs".
- The food story has a family eat "bread, beans and spinach" as a plate with
  all three groups.
- `true_or_not`: "Boiling kills the germs in water" (true); "Stream water
  looks clear, so it has no germs" (false); "We give a sick child no food at
  all" (false); "A child with a danger sign must go to the clinic quickly"
  (true).

## Environment content

Water cycle (sun warms water, vapour rises, cloud of small drops, rain or
snow, streams and ground, again), with a "try it": draw a wet line on a warm
stone and watch it dry. Trees: roots hold soil, leaves break the rain, the
bare hill loses its soil, plant a sapling for every tree cut. Waste: peel and
leaves rot and feed the soil, plastic, bottle and tin do not; rubbish pit away
from water and house; reuse a clean bottle. A rainbow after rain is called
کمان رستم.
